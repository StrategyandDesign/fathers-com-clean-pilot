RETURNING HOME · HUNTSVILLE, ARKANSAS

Come home present.

A private path for the men of Returning Home, built with Fathers.com.

Your family is not waiting for a perfect man. They are waiting for you. This path starts tonight, from where you are.

How it works

01 · Know where you stand. The Profile takes about eight minutes. Your answers are private.

02 · Work a plan. Twelve weeks of small moves, built from your answers.

03 · Train on film. Coming Home Present: one short film each week, then a checkpoint and a lived practice, with your facilitator available for questions.

When you finish, you hold a Certificate of Completion with a serial and a public page anyone can check. It is yours, and it is free.

Private and free

Your results belong to you. Your facilitator sees your progress, never your private answers. Every course and the certificate cost you nothing.
