# Organization admin quick start

Your window is org.html: seats, invites, cohorts, and participation counts.
By design you never see an individual man's answers or scores.

1. Your organization carries a serial (NCF-O) on the public registry, status
   always shown. Your certification is annual and revocable; that is what
   makes it worth something to a court or funder.
2. Facilitators run cohorts; you fund and schedule them. Two to three
   credentialed facilitators per site keeps cohorts continuous.
3. Seats and materials are covered by sponsorship or your budget; the man
   pays nothing, ever. Spend incentive money where drop-off peaks: sessions
   one and two, and the ceremony.
4. The Efficacy Report page shows your funders cohort movement, never a man.
   One page per cohort.
5. Referrals: hand the parent insert to every referring professional. The
   caseworker one-pager answers the court side. Both are in this kit.
