# Forum-moderator review checklist

Quiet partner-kit list for a bonded group or chief executive officer forum. Badge Shared 1-1.117. Not Shared 2.

`vertical_pack_optimization` stays off until Super-admin turns it on. Rehab organizations never receive this pack. There is no public go-to-market until this list is signed off.

## Confidentiality defaults

- [ ] Join is invitation-only. The invite code is shared with invited men only.
- [ ] Leaders see completion flags. Written answers stay off the desk unless a separate org flag unlocks them.
- [ ] Leader notes are not exported. Reports and the quality improvement packet stay flags and completion only.
- [ ] Father chrome on that organization omits the Rehab participation label.

## Non-clinical copy

- [ ] Copy names training and follow-through. It does not name therapy, grades, or a clinical chart on father chrome.
- [ ] Assessments stay educational self-report tools.
- [ ] Certificates record completion. They are not a clinical finding.

## No answer dump

- [ ] Custom assessment answers are not attached to an export.
- [ ] Reviewers stay on cohort totals.
- [ ] The commitment board shows practice completion flags only.

## Sponsorship and deferral

- [ ] The organization funds facilitator capacity. There are no paid participant tiers.
- [ ] Films are unchanged. There is no second app.
- [ ] Public go-to-market stays deferred until this checklist is signed off.
