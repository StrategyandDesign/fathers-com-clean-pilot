# Supervised first cohort: the coaching instrument

The supervised first cohort is not a gate to pass; it is where facilitator
quality, the platform's dominant retention variable, gets built. The NCF
supervisor observes at least sessions one, three, and the final, and coaches
against this list.

## Session one
1. Every man greeted by name at the door.
2. The zero-cost promise said out loud.
3. Every man leaves with a Profile started and one stated commitment.
4. Next session confirmed before dismissal.

## Mid-cohort (session three)
1. Wins opened the room; every man reported.
2. Practice time exceeded lecture time.
3. Any absent man was called the same day he missed. Ask for the story.
4. Attendance trend reviewed against session one.

## The final
1. Finals read personally, feedback given man by man.
2. Ceremony scheduled before program exit, room and guests arranged.
3. Verification sheet produced for any requiring coordinator.

## Credential decision
Credential issues when the supervisor has seen the rhythm hold without
prompting. One coaching note per session, in writing, kept simple. The
renewal conversation a year later starts from these notes.
