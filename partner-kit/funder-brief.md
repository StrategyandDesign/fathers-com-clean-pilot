# Funder brief: completion and operations, not a rating

For donors and foundations. Not a public website page. Not father-facing copy.
This is the only partner-kit sales note that names the Title IV-E Prevention
Services Clearinghouse or the Family First Prevention Services Act.
docs/product/POSITIONING.md and docs/product/EVIDENCE-BAR.md govern claims.
If this brief and those files disagree, those files win.

---

## The honest sentence

Fathers.com is not a Title IV-E Prevention Services Clearinghouse-rated
prevention program. It is not Family First Prevention Services Act drawdown
ready. We do not sell clinical efficacy, Military and Family Life Counseling
approval, Building Strong and Ready Teams approval, reunification readiness,
or proven risk reduction.

What we sell is completion and operations: a fathering course, a named Leader,
and a Certificate of Completion a court or funder can check. The man never
gets a bill.

## What a gift pays for

- Films and the Leader credential
- Checkable certificates
- Cohort counts a grant officer can attach (starts, finishes, certificates)
- The measurement spine a later comparison-group study would need

A gift does not buy a Clearinghouse rating, Title IV-E reimbursement, or a
clinical endpoint.

## Research citations

Cioffi 2023 and similar papers are content-shape analogs only. They describe
how fatherhood programs in the field are built (short skill practice, a
check-in, someone who stays with the man). They are never this product's
trial, efficacy evidence, or rating file. Do not borrow National Fatherhood
Initiative or Cioffi as the Fathers.com study.

## If they ask

- Are you rated? No. Sell completion and operations.
- Can we draw down federal child-welfare reimbursement? No. There is no
  Clearinghouse rating.
- Is there a Military and Family Life Counseling approval on record? No.
- Is there a Building Strong and Ready Teams approval on record? No.
- Does this prove reunification or risk reduction? No.
