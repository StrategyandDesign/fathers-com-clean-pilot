# Certificate ceremony script

Ten minutes, before program exit, in front of the cohort. The ceremony is a
retention and completion instrument; treat it as part of the curriculum.

1. The frame, spoken by the facilitator: "Every man in this room did
   verified work. Hours logged, checkpoints passed, a final read and
   approved. What I hand each man tonight can be confirmed by anyone he
   chooses, in ten seconds, for the rest of his life."
2. Call each man by full name. Hand him the certificate. Read the serial out
   loud. Shake his hand. Let the room respond.
3. One sentence per man from the facilitator: one specific thing this man
   did in the cohort. Specific beats grand.
4. Each man, one sentence: what he will do with it. Optional, offered not
   forced.
5. Close: "The serial on this paper is his. The work behind it is his.
   Verify it anytime at the address printed on it."

Invite family when the setting allows. A man's children seeing him honored
is the point of the whole platform.
