# Certified Facilitator quick start

The evidence is blunt: the facilitator is the retention product. In the
published attrition research, one facilitator ran nine courses with zero
dropouts. This page is the rhythm that produces that.

## Before the cohort
1. Claim every man by his sign-in email from your Desk (lead.html). Claims
   cost him nothing and unlock his enrollment.
2. Confirm materials and the room. Same place, same hour, every week.
3. Send each man a direct, personal invitation. Named invitations outperform
   flyers; men come when a man they respect asks them by name.

## Session one, the beats
1. Greet each man by name at the door.
2. Say the price out loud: the Profile, the plan, the course, and the
   Certificate are free to him, always.
3. Every man takes or reviews his Keystone Profile. His gap domain becomes
   his plan.
4. Each man states one commitment for the week. Write them down.
5. Confirm next week's time before anyone leaves.

## The weekly rhythm
1. Open with wins. Every man reports on last week's step, no shame attached.
2. Teach the session. Short. The session is for practice, not lecture.
3. Practice in pairs. Men keep coming back for the men, not the material.
4. Each man leaves with one step. One.
5. Confirm attendance for next week before the room empties.

## When a man is in distress
See facilitator-triage-and-referral.md in this kit. The short version: watch
behavior, make the warm handoff the same day, tell your organization lead,
and never carry it alone.

## When a man misses
Call him the same day. Not a text, a call. No shame, one question: what would
make next week possible. Attendance recovers when absence is noticed within
hours, not weeks.

## The final and the ceremony
He writes the final; you read it and approve. The certificate issues with his
serial. Present it in front of the room, before program exit, with the men
who trained beside him. The ceremony is part of the curriculum.

## Your desk
Claims, cohort life, and the verification sheet live at lead.html. The
verification sheet is one CSV for any coordinator who requires proof.

You lead education. You do not diagnose, counsel, or treat, and the
credential never implies you do.
