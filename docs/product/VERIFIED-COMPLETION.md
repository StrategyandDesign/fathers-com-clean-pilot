# Verified-only completion (locked)

Product rule as of August 2026. Optimized for organizations that pay for certification.

Pilot Desk today issues **Leader-issued completion proof** after every session in a training is done. Public serial check is live. A claim-gated mint is wired and stays behind `certificates_require_claim` (environment `CERTIFICATES_REQUIRE_CLAIM`, default off).

## What any man can do free
- Keystone Profile
- Twelve-week plan
- Course **preview** (try the player flow; not proof)
- Public serial check at `/verify` and `/verify/{serial}`

## What a claimed seat adds
- Official enrollment recorded on `participant_claims`
- Certificate mint when `certificates_require_claim` is on
- Facilitator review of the final (when that desk ships)

Until the flag is on, a Leader may still issue completion proof after full session completion. The serial is checkable either way. The printable document is completion-only and carries the misuse disclaimer.

## Hard boundaries
- Preview never mints a serial
- Preview never says the man "completed" the certificate for proof purposes
- A private or preview sitting cannot be upgraded into a serial later; verified work happens under a claimed seat when the flag is on
- Organizations sell accountability and checkable proof, not exclusive access to the film ideas
- Certificates do not find court fitness, reunification safety, or clinical treatment

## Participant line
Train free. Earn proof through a Certified Organization.

## Organization line
Men can start on their own. Claimed seats mint certificates a funder can check when `certificates_require_claim` is on. Until then, Leaders issue completion proof with a public serial.
