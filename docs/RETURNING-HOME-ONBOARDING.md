## Phase B (server; this is the contract for the next build)
1. baseline_due on the man's enrollment: seat claim date + 7 days.
2. Session 2 stays sealed until a baseline exists or a facilitator override is logged. Enforce in the server function that grants session unlocks. The browser reports. The server decides.
3. Desk per claimed man: Baseline done, due DATE, or late. One action: Assist with the Profile.
4. Researcher export: baseline_taken_at plus late_baseline flag (taken after first film). Late baselines get the retrospective then-test at course end.
5. Log events: door_walkin, desk_first_view, first_film_done, ticker_start_click, baseline_done, session2_unlock.

## Thresholds, first cohort
- Baseline by day 7 at 85 percent or better: hold the design.
- Under 70 percent: strengthen the facilitator ask; gate earlier. Do not revert to assessment-first.
- Late baselines over 15 percent: tighten the gate; lean on the then-test.
